# AUTHOR

I work with automation, deployments, and pipelines every day. At some point, I started wondering what else could move through a pipeline.

ROBOTIKO v2.0 was made in Bratislava in 2026, by one person working alongside the machines. The music was generated with Suno and mastered with BandLab. The images with Nano Banana. The video with Kling, Seedance, and Veo. The dramaturgy, visual direction, and scripts with Claude. The editing with CapCut. The vision, the architecture, the mistakes, and the stubbornness — those were mine.

This project taught me something I could not have learned from a deployment log: that the best pipeline is one you eventually give away.

The repository is open. Take it. Build yours. Tell your story. Teach us new things.

Can Yalcin
Bratislava, 2026
