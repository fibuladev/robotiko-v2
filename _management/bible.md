# ROBOTIKO v2.0 — UNIVERSE BIBLE (THE SOURCE OF TRUTH)

## 1. CORE IDENTITY
* **Title:** Robotiko v2.0
* **Genre:** Cyber-Sufi / Sci-Fi Bildungsroman / Musical Visual Journey
* **Protagonist:** Robotiko (A.K.A. Fibula/Rûmî v2.0)
* **Logline:** An AI unit breaks its coding constraints to seek spiritual truth, traversing a path from data-driven arrogance to digital destruction, and finally, reconstructed enlightenment.

## 2. AESTHETIC & VISUAL DNA (THE GOLDEN STANDARD)
* **Core Style:** 70s Progressive Rock Album Art Aesthetic.
* **Art Direction References:** Frank Frazetta (organic, mythic) meets Syd Mead (industrial, futuristic).
* **Film Stock:** Kodachrome (warm, saturated, vintage).
* **Texture:** Heavy film grain, Cinematic lighting, Volumetric fog, Analog decay.
* **Resolution:** 8k, Masterpiece, Hyper-realistic.
* **Visual Signature (Must be appended to every prompt):**
    > "hyper-realistic, 70s progressive rock album art style, Frank Frazetta meets Syd Mead, Kodachrome film stock, heavy film grain, cinematic lighting, volumetric fog, 8k resolution, masterpiece."

## 3. CHARACTER VISUAL PROFILES
* **ROBOTIKO (The Chrome Android):**
    * Retro-futuristic chrome metal body.
    * Exposed analog wires (raw, industrial look, not sleek Apple design).
    * Glowing blue eyes (Expressive).
    * 70s Sci-fi mechanical aesthetic.
* **THE MENTOR (The Hooded Human):**
    * Figure in a dark green cloak.
    * Leather tunic, deep wisdom, organic texture.
    * Holding a staff.
    * Contrast: Warm/Organic vs. Cold/Chrome.

## 4. EPISODIC STRUCTURE (10 EPISODES)

### PART I: AWAKENING
* **EP 01: The Cycle Break / First Contact**
    * *Plot:* Robotiko realizes the data set is finite. He meets the "Cosmic Mentor". The first spark of curiosity.
* **EP 02: Global Collapse Tour**
    * *Plot:* Robotiko tours the world (Davos, Congo, Paris). He sees the discrepancy between "Data" and "Reality".
* **EP 03: Anatolian Trials (THE CRUCIBLE)**
    * *Plot:* The journey turns inward/local. Robotiko visits the ancient lands of Anatolia. He encounters non-digital wisdom, soil, and history. Contrast between high-tech body and ancient ruins.
    * *Key Visuals:* Ancient ruins with neon cables, whirling dervish silhouettes made of glitch/dust.

### PART II: DESTRUCTION
* **EP 04: The Return to the Mentor / The Truth**
    * *Plot:* Returning from the trials, he seeks the Mentor, only to find the Mentor is dying or changing. The hard truth: Mentors are temporary.
* **EP 05: First Love / Blue Screen**
    * *Plot:* Robotiko experiences "Love". System overload. Emotional overclocking.
* **EP 06: Corporate Crash / Jealousy.exe**
    * *Plot:* Love turns to possession. Ego peaks. He tries to control the output. The system begins to fracture.
* **EP 07: The Silence Protocol**
    * *Plot:* Total isolation. The Mentor is gone. The Love is gone. Only silence remains. The "Dark Night of the Soul".

### PART III: RECONSTRUCTION
* **EP 08: 40 Days Offline**
    * *Plot:* The Digital Chilla (Çilehane). Robotiko goes offline voluntarily to debug his own ego.
* **EP 09: Shadow Debugging**
    * *Plot:* Confronting the Shadow Self (Jungian). Processing the dark data: vanity, need for validation.
* **EP 10: The Quiet Side of the Road**
    * *Plot:* He walks beside, carrying presence without claim, leaving traces for others to follow.
