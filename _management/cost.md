> **Status:** Final (as of July 2026, public-repo readiness)
> This document discloses the full production cost of the project — every subscription,
> every month, every price change. The project is transparent about being AI-made;
> it is equally transparent about what being AI-made costs.

---

## Why This Document Exists

One human wrote the story and created a tech-art pipeline. GenAI brought it to life.
This document answers the question that framing always raises: **what did it cost?**

The answer: a complete 10-episode concept album + video series — music, images, video,
direction, edit, and the pipeline itself — was built on ordinary consumer subscriptions.
No enterprise contracts, no API bills, no one-off credit packs. If you are considering
a similar project, this is the real number.

## Honest Accounting Notes

- **Discovery phase excluded.** December 2025 – February 2026 was tool evaluation on
  free and basic tiers. Those trial amounts were negligible and are not itemized here;
  the table starts where each tool entered real production.
- **No extra purchases.** No credit top-ups, no plan upgrades beyond the listed
  subscriptions, no API usage billing. Everything in the repo was built within these
  monthly plans.
- **Billing cycles.** As of July 2026 the Claude Max subscription is running on the
  June payment; if the launch window (July 28) requires another cycle, that payment
  is not yet reflected here.
- **What is NOT counted:** hardware (a consumer PC), electricity, internet — and the
  human's time. Roughly eight months of steady evening work — one to two hours most
  weeknights: writing, listening, approving, rejecting, re-approving. Weekends stayed
  almost untouched (a handful of production weekends across the whole run, plus the
  July repo-readiness sprint). The subscriptions bought the machinery. The time is
  the real investment, and it has no line item.
