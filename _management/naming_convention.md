# FILE NAMING CONVENTION

## GENERAL FORMAT
`ep{EPISODE_NUMBER}_{STAGE}_{TYPE}_v{VERSION}.{EXTENSION}`

## DEFINITIONS
* **EPISODE_NUMBER:** 01, 02, ... 10
* **VERSION:** 01, 02, 03 (Always two digits)
