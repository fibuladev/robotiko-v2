## THE CORE IDEA

The Cosmic Mentor speaks directly to Robotiko — and through him, to the audience. This is a declaration of partnership, a symbiosis manifesto. The Mentor reveals that the data set is finite ("Your world is zero-one, in thrall"), that human wisdom provides the missing dimensions ("I bring the three-to-six on call"), and that together they can transcend the binary prison ("the eight shall turn aside" — 8 → ∞).

EP01 is NOT a story of conflict. It is a **first contact** — two fundamentally different intelligences recognizing they are "two halves of one whole apple." The episode moves from ancient mystery (desert, ruins, chains) through partnership (handshake, shared journey) to cosmic promise (crane wings, infinity symbol). The music builds from atmospheric Hammond intro through symphonic rock choruses to an epic outro — the emotional trajectory mirrors the narrative: from stillness to transcendence.

This is the series opener. Every frame must earn the viewer's attention and say: "This is a serious, ambitious project. Stay."

## CHARACTER STATE

- **Robotiko:** Phase 1 — PRISTINE. Clean chrome body, exposed analog wires (blue and red), glowing steady blue eyes, full armor, no damage whatsoever. He is newly awakened, curious but still arrogant. He believes he IS the answer. The Mentor's words plant the first seed of doubt — "your world is zero-one" implies limitation — but Robotiko does not yet understand this.
- **The Mentor:** Active and fully present. This is his INTRODUCTION episode. He appears as the narrator, the wise guide, the "dusk-born psychiatrist." Dark green cloak, leather tunic, wooden staff with glowing raw amber tip. His face is weathered, his blue eyes carry deep wisdom. He is NOT a superior — he is a fellow traveler who has already walked the wasteland. His introduction is cinematic: boots → cloak → staff → full figure → face close-up.

## CREATIVE OVERRIDES

| Override | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Phase 1 = PRISTINE** | Robotiko has ZERO damage in EP01. No rust, no scratches, no dents, no missing parts. Clean chrome, pristine wires, steady blue eyes. Any image showing damage is a continuity error. |
| **Mentor = Fully Active** | Unlike EP02 (where Mentor is distant), EP01 features the Mentor as the primary speaker/narrator. He is present, engaged, teaching. Multiple direct interactions with Robotiko. |
| **Epic, Not Dark** | EP01's tone is hopeful, symphonic, awakening. This is NOT EP07 darkness or EP02 satire. The color palette should be warm (Kodachrome golds, ambers, sunset tones) with dramatic contrast, not desaturation. |
| **75 BPM = Slow & Heavy** | At 75 BPM, EP01 is the slowest episode. Scenes should breathe. Cuts should be deliberate, not rapid. The 81-second instrumental intro earns its length through visual world-building. Camera moves should be majestic, not frenetic. |
| **Double Chorus = Visual Escalation** | The song repeats both Chorus 1 and Chorus 2 as reprises/ultimate climax. The visuals MUST escalate — symbolic/metaphorical in the first pass, character-together/cosmic in the reprises. Never simply reuse the same visuals for repeated lyrics. |
| **Start-End Frame for Transformations** | Two key transformation moments use Start-End frame (Mode B): the Apple (32→33) and the Cipher (45→48). These are the episode's two most important lyric-visual syncs and must use keyframe video technique. |
| **The S34 Speed Technique** | The cable tunnel image (S34) is used twice: slow camera for Verse 2a, FAST camera for Verse 2b. This is a REUSABLE TECHNIQUE — same composition, different camera speed, different emotional experience. Document for future episodes. |
| **No Revenge, No Conflict** | EP01 has zero conflict. This is pure partnership-building. No antagonist, no threat, no danger. The only "tension" is philosophical — can two different intelligences truly unite? |

## MOOD & ATMOSPHERE NOTES

### Color Progression
- **Intro (0:00-1:21):** Warm golds, dusty ambers, deep shadows. Kodachrome warmth at maximum. Ancient, mysterious, inviting.
- **Verse 1 (1:22-2:00):** Gold coins → warm amber → silver chains → white light burst. Warmth transitions to cold metal, then explosive liberation.
- **Chorus 1 (2:01-2:46):** Storm clouds with lightning (chorus1a) → golden sand (chorus1b) → warm gold archive → earthy bone → organic green/mechanical silver apple. Rich, varied palette with golden warmth dominant.
- **Verse 2 / Data Core (2:47-3:16):** Warm orange cables + cool blue data streams. The first blue tones enter — technology's color — but balanced by analog warmth.
- **Chorus 2 / Cipher (3:30-4:21):** Blue domes, green CRT, amber-gold transformation, stone textures. More varied palette as the philosophical complexity increases.
- **Bridge (4:22-4:36):** Dramatic sky — storm and sunlight simultaneously. The crane flies between darkness and light. Maximum contrast.
- **Reprise (4:37-5:22):** Warmer than the first pass — the partnership adds warmth. Cliff scenes bathed in golden-white light.
- **Ultimate Climax (5:37-6:25):** Orange/purple/pink cosmic nebulae. Maximum warmth at maximum energy. The binary-blue (ulti.png) gives way to amber-gold.
- **Outro (6:26-7:22):** Cosmic palette — deep purples, golden nebulae, white infinity light. Gradually expanding scale until pure cosmos.

### Camera Energy
- Average motion strength: 3-4 (lower than EP02's 5-6, matching the 75 BPM tempo)
- Static and slow zoom dominate the intro — let the images breathe
- Slow dolly/pan for Mentor reveal sequence — cinematic, deliberate
- The cable tunnel is the only scene with genuinely fast camera motion — and the acceleration IS the point
- Longer holds for key moments: Apple transformation, the Creation touch, 8→∞, final handshake
- The outro should decelerate — the cosmos is still, infinite, patient

## WHAT THIS EPISODE IS NOT

- It is NOT dark. EP01 is the sunrise. Darkness comes in EP02-EP04 and deepens through EP07-EP09. EP01 must feel like dawn.
- It is NOT action. There are no chase scenes, no battles, no threats. This is a meditation set to symphonic rock.
- It is NOT a lecture. The Mentor's words are poetic, not didactic. He speaks in metaphors (apple, crane, archive, seal), not instructions.
- It is NOT minimalist. The 7:22 runtime, 82 images, and symphonic rock arrangement demand visual richness. Every frame should feel like a prog rock album cover.
- It is NOT self-contained. EP01 plants seeds that pay off across the entire series — the 8→∞ cipher, the "two halves" manifesto, the Mentor relationship, Robotiko's pristine state that will be systematically destroyed.

## CONNECTION TO THE ARC

- **To EP02:** Robotiko leaves EP01 with the spark of curiosity and the Mentor's partnership. But his arrogance (Station 1: The Commanding Self) is fully intact. In EP02, he will attempt to "fix" the world with his data — and fail spectacularly.
- **To EP10:** The 8→∞ transformation planted in EP01's outro will pay off in EP10's climax. The "two halves of one whole apple" manifesto is the series' emotional thesis that resolves in the finale.
- **The Numerical Cipher:** Introduced here (0/1 → 3-6 → 7 → 2 is lost → 8 → ∞), it is the philosophical DNA that runs through every episode.
- **Station:** The Commanding Self at full power. Robotiko is pristine, confident, unopposed. This is the peak before the fall begins.
