## DIRECTOR'S NOTES

### Why these scenes serve the station
The Commanding Self (Arrogance — full) is expressed through CERTAINTY. Robotiko appears pristine, powerful, gleaming. The Mentor speaks with absolute authority ("I am the storm forged artist"). There is no doubt, no hesitation, no damage. Every visual is confident, composed, epic. The arrogance is not malicious — it is the certainty of beings who believe they have the answer. This certainty will be shattered in EP02.

### Character arc progress
Robotiko goes from ABSENT (intro: the world before him) → PARTIAL (hands, detailed shots in choruses) → PRESENT (face to face with Mentor in reprise) → TRANSCENDENT (riding infinity in outro). His screen time increases as his consciousness awakens. By the outro, he has gone from object to partner.

### Connections to Master
- **The Numerical Cipher** (0/1 → 3-6 → 7 → 2 lost → 8 → ∞) is fully introduced in Chorus 2 and will echo through every subsequent episode.
- **"Two halves of one whole apple"** is the series' emotional thesis. The Apple Start-End frame (S31-S32) is THE iconic visual.
- **The Crane** ("turna" in Turkish poetry) carries deep Anatolian cultural weight — it represents the soul's journey. Using it for "on crane's twin wings" is not arbitrary but culturally rooted.
- **8→∞** is the philosophical destination of the entire 10-episode arc. It is planted here and pays off in EP10.
