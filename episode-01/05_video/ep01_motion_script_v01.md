## VIDEO STRATEGY REFERENCE

| Mode | When to Use | Input | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A — Standard** | Atmospheric shots, simple movement, no transformation | 1 image | 5s or 10s |
| **B — Start/End Keyframes** | Transformations, morphing, state changes | 2 images | 5s or 10s |

**Duration Coverage Strategy:**

| Scene Duration | Strategy | Clip Count | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| ≤ 5s | **Direct** | 1 × 5s | Trim in CapCut |
| 6–10s | **Direct** | 1 × 10s | Trim in CapCut |
| 11–15s | **Speed Ramp** | 1 × 10s + slow-mo (max 1.5×) | |
| 16–30s | **Multi-Clip** | ⌈duration/10⌉ clips | Each sub-clip own camera move |
| 30s+ | **Multi-Clip** | ⌈duration/10⌉ clips | May need supplementary images |

**Motion Strength Scale:** 1 = Barely breathing / 5 = Cinematic drama / 10 = Chaos
