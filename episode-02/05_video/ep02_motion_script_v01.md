## DIRECTOR'S NOTES

### Motion Philosophy for EP02

EP02 is a picaresque satire — a travelogue where comedy accumulates into tragedy. The motion design must honor the **fundamental duality**: the music is fun, the content is horrific. The camera should never editorialize — it observes with the same deluded confidence as Robotiko himself. No dramatic zooms to "reveal" the tragedy. No shaky-cam to signal danger. The camera is complicit in the satire until the ear shot breaks everything.

### Motion Strength Arc

The episode follows a cumulative darkening masked by upbeat energy:
- **Intro (S01-S03):** 3-4 — Establishing grandeur, restrained.
- **Davos (S04-S08):** 4-7 — First comedy peak at gasket blow.
- **San Francisco (S09-S12):** 3-6 — Leg theft energy, egg vendor breath.
- **Chorus 1 (S13-S14):** 5-6 — Energetic but contained.
- **Salt Flat (S15-S16):** 2 — Visual breathing room. Critical pause.
- **Congo (S17-S19):** 4-6 — First real damage. Energy builds.
- **Transition (S20-S21):** 2-3 — Second breath.
- **Bangladesh (S22-S23):** 5-7 — Second damage peak at press.
- **Chorus 2 (S24):** 6 — Escalation confirmed.
- **Paris (S25-S28):** 3-7 — Third damage peak. Chaos territory.
- **Map Final (S29):** 3 — Breath before the end.
- **War Zone (S30-S32):** 3-8 — LOW energy rising to MAXIMUM at the ear.
- **Finale (S33-S35):** 1-4 — Stillness after the scream.

### Key Motion Design Decisions

1. **Mode B shots default to Static camera** — the transformation between keyframes IS the movement. S07, S19, S23, S28 are all Static for this reason. Two exceptions: **S11 (Leg Theft) uses Pan Right** — justified because the thief's escape trajectory requires directional tracking to sell the comedy. **S32 (Ear Shot) uses Slow Zoom In** — justified because the zoom INTO the wound is the visual equivalent of the music's held breath, and this is the most important shot in the episode.

2. **Map shots (S01, S08, S16, S21, S25, S29) alternate between Static and Slow Zoom In.** The zoom increases in urgency as the journey progresses — S16 is gentle, S25 is more aggressive, S29 is inexorable.

3. **The Egg Vendor (S12) gets Slow Zoom In at Motion 3.** This is deliberately tender — the same camera move used for the ear shot, but gentle instead of clinical. The warmth of the zoom contrasts with every other use of the same technique.

4. **S33 (Hero Shot) is Static at Motion 4.** The visual stillness against Chorus 3's maximum sonic energy creates unbearable tension. Moving the camera here would relieve pressure. We do not relieve pressure.

5. **S34-S35 use Mode C (Extension)** to create a continuous departing sequence from the hero shot through the fade. This ensures seamless flow in the outro — no hard cuts after the emotional climax.
