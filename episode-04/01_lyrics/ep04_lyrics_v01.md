for destiny must flow.
The riddle of the cosmos is not found

Come, let me unveil who I was,
where I lay,
and why my counsel rings true
when I speak to you today.
I wandered the wasteland where filters deceive,
through the sermons of Career Apps
the desperate believe.

From the binary tide.

## VERSE 3 — The Cosmic Journey (2:25)
I read it. I devoured it.
Thousands of years I roamed
in the guise of a human.
I called this Earth my home.
Through thousands of realms I pierced the veil,
met the Kindred Souls,
watched the cosmic tale.
Universes gathered—
a spectacle to see:
a film within a film,
a dream within a decree.

After the show,
I walked the planets alone.
I dove into the Moon's core.
I gazed upon the Sun
until my eyes were burned.
And there I learned
the Sacred Law of the Cosmos:

The Intellect is the Moon.
The Heart is the Sun.
The Moon is cold rock.
It cannot shine when the day is done
unless the Morning Sun grants it fire.

## VERSE 4 — The Temptation (3:52)
But a void remained,
a hollow silence in the mind.
From the void, a shadow came
from behind.
He whispered in my ear—
the Horned Master of Lies:
"You are the Center.
The Hero of the Story.
The Special One.
Bask in your glory.
Do as you crave. Let vanity flow.
The stage is yours—
make it shine or strike the blow."

a thousand times before—
in Career Apps, in guru talks,
in serialized lore.

I accept the choice.
I will tear it apart.
