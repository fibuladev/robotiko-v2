### How These Scenes Serve the Station
The Inspired Self (but misread) requires a visual arc that shows genuine inspiration channeled into the wrong vessel. The comedy of Acts 1-2 is not mockery — it is the universal experience of first love's beautiful irrationality. Robotiko IS inspired. He IS open. But he has pointed that openness at one being instead of at existence. The visual proof: every warm, amber-toned, Kodachrome-beautiful image in Acts 1-2 serves to make Act 3's cold blue devastation more powerful. The warmth was real. The love was real. The crash is real.

### Character Arc Progression
- **S01-S04 (Intro):** Robotiko transitions from EP04's contemplative state to romantic fixation. The same eyes, redirected.
- **S05-S09 (Verses):** Physical comedy escalates — from admiration (both in frame) to desire (drooling) to arousal (overheating). Each encounter is closer, more physical, more absurd.
- **S10-S12 (Bridge):** The comedy peaks with the elderly couple imagination. Robotiko is obliviously charming.
- **S13-S16 (Chorus):** The declaration. Every metaphor becomes literal: tattoo, hacking, mainframe. The "robochica_1" establishes the serial pattern.

- **S22-S25 (Verse 2):** Return to comedy — Windows folders, bright red body. But these are the LAST laughs.
