### Camera Angle Rule
When a character is **seated at a desk facing a monitor**, the prompt MUST specify a non-frontal angle: `profile`, `three-quarter rear`, `seen from behind`, or `over-the-shoulder`. The character reference shows a front-facing pose — if the prompt says "sitting at desk" without specifying the viewing angle, the generator will place the character facing the camera instead of the monitor.
