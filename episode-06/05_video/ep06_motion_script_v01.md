## DIRECTOR'S NOTES

### Motion Strength Strategy
Average MS: **3.8** — deliberately lower than the Destruction-phase norm (5-7). EP06 is the "Tranquil Self — Broken": not explosive destruction but exhausted rebellion. The energy lives in proximity escalation and the weight of silence, not in camera chaos. Peaks at MS 7 (S18 Standing Up, S21 Face to Face) land on Verse 3 and Refrain 3 — the episode's emotional climax. The 42s guitar solo outro (S42-S43) drops to MS 1-3, letting the indifferent architecture carry the devastation.

### Key Decisions

1. **S12-S13-S14 Bathroom Sequence:** Location change from office to corporate bathroom. V03 rewrote these scenes: S12 = bathroom doorway entry, S13 = hands gripping stall partition, S14 = face in bathroom mirror. Return to office at S15. The bathroom is where the breakdown happens invisibly — what the office never sees.

2. **S15 Front View Override:** V03 rewrote S15 from rear view to front view: "Both androids seated side by side at adjacent desks, both facing the camera." This overrides the Camera Angle Rule for desk scenes — the human explicitly chose this framing for the Refrain 2 contrast shot.

3. **S38 Mode B → Mode A Downgrade:** Dual action risk (Robotiko walking + Worker head turn) likely produces weird morphing mid-states per the MODE B KEYFRAME LIMITS lesson (EP05 S15). Downgraded to Mode A using S38b (end frame — Robotiko mid-corridor, worker head turned). S38a image available for alternative takes if needed.

4. **S41 Mode B Retained:** The seated → kneeling collapse is a single clear action in a stable environment — the exact use case where Mode B works. One subject, one transformation, same composition.

5. **S37 Composite Treatment:** Split-frame image (damaged vs. pristine chest plates). Following COMPOSITE/SPLIT-WORLD lesson: describe what IS, not what HAPPENS. Static camera, locked composition, atmospheric micro-elements only.

6. **S42-S43 Multi-Clip Pull-Back:** 42s of guitar solo covered by 4 sub-clips with varied camera moves: Slow Zoom Out → Pan Right → Slow Zoom Out → Static. A gradual departure from the collapsed figure, ending in near-darkness.
