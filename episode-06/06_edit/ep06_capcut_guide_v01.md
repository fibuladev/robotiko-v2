## 9. COLOR REFERENCE

### Reference Clip
**S07 — The Perfect Worker Introduction** (0:55–1:07, Kling 3.0)

**Why S07:** Well-lit character close-up under cold fluorescent light with clean chrome surface. Establishes the Kodachrome baseline for the cold office — warm enough to feel vintage, cold enough to feel sterile. The pristine titanium surface provides a neutral reference for color matching across all 45 clips.

### Tool-Specific Corrections

After applying the Kodachrome LUT and Color Match, review these tool-specific tendencies:

| Tool | Tendency | Correction |
|------|----------|------------|
| **Kling 3.0** (25 clips) | Slightly cool blue cast | Boost warmth via HSL: Orange +5%, Amber +5% |
| **Kling 2.5 Turbo** (6 clips) | Neutral — usually matches LUT well | Minimal or no correction needed |
| **Seedance 1.0** (14 clips) | Generally good LUT match, occasional slight over-saturation | If oversaturated: reduce Saturation -5% in HSL |

### EP06-Specific Color Notes

- **Cold is intentional.** EP06's office has all warmth stripped from EP05. Do NOT push warmth globally. The Kodachrome LUT at 80% provides the vintage texture without reintroducing the warmth that the narrative removed.
- **Amber scenes (S03, S19, S33):** These three scenes contain the only warm color in the episode. After Color Match, verify the amber elements retain their warmth — they should stand out against the cold blue-white base. If Color Match neutralizes them, manually boost Orange/Amber in HSL for these three clips only.
- **Smoke scenes (S30, S31):** Verify the haze reads as industrial (grey-white) not atmospheric (warm golden). If the LUT pushes smoke toward warm amber, desaturate the haze zones via Curves in these clips only.
- **S37 (Texture Comparison):** Split-frame clip — both halves should share the same Kodachrome base. Verify the "damaged" side and "pristine" side don't have drastically different color temperatures after correction.

### Verification Method
Scrub through the full timeline at 1× speed. At every cut point, the color temperature should feel continuous. Pay special attention to:
- Office → Bathroom transitions (S11→S12, S14→S15): lighting change is narrative, but color temperature should remain consistent
- Office → Meeting Room transition (S35→S36): different environment, same cold palette
- Tool transitions: clips from different tools placed adjacent (e.g., Kling S10 → Seedance S11) should not have visible color jumps
