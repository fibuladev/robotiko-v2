## DIRECTOR'S NOTES

- **Why these scenes serve the station (The Surrendering Self / Dark Night):** The structure is a
  literal descent — twilight → night, posture collapsing (bench-sit → walk → sit → desk → bed), camera
  retreating one rung per refrain — to the absolute bottom (S22 cry, S23 the speck in the void). Then a
  *chosen* surrender: he lets go of the old self (S25 unplug) and, for the first time, decides to move
  (S26–S29). Surrender as doorway, not grave.
