## VIDEO STRATEGY REFERENCE

| Mode | When to Use | Input | Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A -- Standard** | Atmospheric shots, simple movement, no transformation | 1 image | 5s or 10s |
| **B -- Start/End Keyframes** | Single clear action between two locked compositions | 2 images | 5s or 10s |

**Duration Coverage Strategy:**

| Scene Duration | Strategy | Clip Count | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| <= 5s | **Direct** | 1 x 5s | Trim excess in CapCut |
| 6-10s | **Direct** | 1 x 10s | Trim in CapCut |
| 11-15s | **Speed Ramp** | 1 x 10s + slow-mo (max 1.5x) | Slowdown ratio noted per clip |
| 16-30s | **Multi-Clip** | ceil(duration / 10) clips | Each sub-clip gets own camera move |
